A closer look at Vanilla

The pitch: Vanilla Rec Room

Rec Room shut down. Vanilla is here.

I found Vanilla while looking for another place to play and create. It's a free, cross-platform social game with player-made rooms, team games, and ways to play on a screen or in VR.

FREE TO PLAY  ·  PC + META QUEST  ·  SCREEN OR VR

A little Vanilla field guide

Pick a topic to explore.

These details come from Vanilla's official website.

A social game with lots of rooms

Vanilla says it's free and cross-platform, so friends can hang out across devices. It can be played on a screen or with a VR headset.

Play styleSocial rooms, games, and shared activities
CostFree, according to Vanilla's site

Compete or team up

For competitive games, Vanilla highlights paintball with Capture the Flag and Team Battle, plus different maps and customization.

For co-op, team up with friends in the Jumbotron quest and take on its robot enemies together.

Explore rooms or make one

Vanilla's site describes thousands of player-created rooms, with hundreds more being added daily. You can also build rooms with friends.

That means there are places to explore and creative projects to try, not just one fixed activity.

Try it on Quest or PC

Meta QuestQuest 2, Quest Pro, and Quest 3 are listed on the official download page.
PCVanilla lists Windows 7, 10, or 11, with screen and VR play.
